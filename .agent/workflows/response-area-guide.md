# CalPOP Response Area & Reference Hub Guide

The **Response Area** is a high-efficiency workspace designed for sponsors to process incoming prisoner correspondence and draft accurate, context-aware responses.

## Key Interface Components

### 1. Persistent Identity Header
Always visible at the top of the workspace, displaying:
- **Prisoner Name & Facility**: Immediate context on who you are responding to.
- **Archive REF (CPID) & CDCR Number**: Side-by-side display of internal archival IDs and public identification numbers to ensure records matching.

### 2. Multi-Pane Navigation
The workspace is divided into three primary functional areas:
- **Compose**: A minimalist, distraction-free markdown editor for drafting your response. Includes a word/character counter and secure auto-save indicators.
- **Reference Hub**: An intelligent library system for curriculum materials and letter exchange history.
- **Incoming Letter**: A side-by-side verification pane showing the **Original Scan Artifact** (2/3 width) and the **Digital Transcription** (1/3 width).

---

## Formatting & Final Output

The Response Station uses **Markdown** for lightweight, efficient drafting. 
- **Digital Preview**: Use the "Check Preview" toggle to see a high-fidelity simulation of your response.
- **Printed Variance**: Note that digital rendering may vary slightly from the final professionally printed correspondence. 
- **Proofreading**: Always perform a final proofread of your text in the "Edit Source" view or "Check Preview" before archival.

---

## The Reference Hub Workbench

The Reference Hub features a "Workbench" system that eliminates constant file-system navigation by allowing you to pin documents to an active queue.

### Prisoner Sponsee Search (Exchange History Search)
The Reference Hub includes an interactive search field entitled **"Prisoner Sponsee (searchable)"** which controls the automatic filtering of the **Letter Exchange History** tab.
- **Emerald Text (Auto-Scouting)**: The system is proactively reading the incoming letter. If it finds a matching Archive ID (CPID), it will automatically lock onto it and filter your history.
- **Amber Text (Manual Override)**: If you manually type an ID into this field, it will turn Amber to indicate you have taken control. This allows you to "scout" any other prisoner's history for research without leaving your draft.
- **Reset to Auto**: Click the "Reset to Auto" button to clear your search and return to the system's detected ID instantly.

### Curriculum Workbench (6 Slot Limit)
- Pin up to **6 educational or policy documents** simultaneously.
- Switch between them using the sub-tab bar at the top of the curriculum view.
- Perfect for referencing templates, guidelines, and multiple course materials at once.

### Exchange History Workbench (4 Slot Limit)
- Pin up to **4 historical letters or records** from the prisoner's archive.
- Allows you to maintain context of previous conversations while drafting new ones.

### Features
- **One-Click Insert**: For `.docx` artifacts, use the "Insert into Response" button to pull text directly into your draft.
- **Instant Toggling**: Your "Library Browser" remains active in the background, allowing you to easily swap new documents into your workbench.
- **Visual Status**: The tab bar indicates how many slots are remaining in your current workbench category.
