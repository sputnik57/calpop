# Sponsor Manual - CalPOP Volunteer Portal

## Welcome
Thank you for volunteering with CalPOP! This portal allows you to receive letters from your assigned sponsees and write responses safely and easily.

## 1. Getting Started
### Logging In
1. Navigate to the Sponsor Portal URL.
2. Click **"Login with Microsoft"**.
3. Use your personal email that was registered with CalPOP.

### The Dashboard
Your **"Unified Inbox"** shows:
*   **New Assignments**: Letters waiting for your reply.
*   **In Progress**: Drafts you are working on.
*   **Completed**: Letters you have sent in the past.

## 2. Reading a Letter
1. Click on a letter in your **Inbox**.
2. You will see the **Redacted Version** of the prisoner's letter.
   *   *Note: Personal names and addresses are blacked out for safety.*
3. You can view the original scanned image (redacted) or the typed text version.

## 3. Writing a Response
1. Click **"Reply"** to open the Editor.
2. **Compose your message**. You can use formatting like bold/italics.
   *   *Tip: The system autosaves your draft every few seconds.*
3. **Attachments**: If you want to include an educational article or resource, click "Attach File" (PDFs only).

## 4. Submitting for Review
1. When finished, review your letter.
2. Click **"Submit for Review"**.
3. The status will change to **"Submitted"**.
4. An Admin will review the content to ensure it meets facility guidelines.

## 5. Revisions (If Needed)
If an Admin spots an issue (e.g., a rule violation):
1. You will receive a notification: **"Revisions Requested"**.
2. Open the letter to see the **Admin's Comment**.
3. Edit your response to address the feedback.
4. Click **"Resubmit"**.

## 6. Privacy & Safety
*   **Anonymity**: The prisoner never sees your real name or address. All mail is routed through the CalPOP office.
*   **Data Security**: Do not download letters to your personal device. Read and reply strictly within this secure portal.
